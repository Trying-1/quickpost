## Style Guidelines
- Simplicity: 1–2 shapes; must read at 48×48 px
- High contrast: Bold mark on flat background
- Rounded corners: Matches Android adaptive icon style
- No text in the icon; avoid microscopic details

## Color Recommendations
- Primary: Electric blue, coral red, teal, or purple gradients
- Backgrounds: Dark navy or white for contrast
- Accent: Small spark/arrow for the "quick" motion feel

## Export Specs
- Master: 1024×1024
- Android Adaptive Icon:
  - Foreground: transparent PNG/SVG (main symbol)
  - Background: solid color or image
- Safe area: Keep main mark within ~66–72% of canvas
